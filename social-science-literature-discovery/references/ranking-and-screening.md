# Ranking and Screening

## Separate dimensions

Never compress ranking into an unexplained total score. Preserve:

- topic relevance;
- research-object relevance;
- measurement relevance;
- method relevance;
- temporal novelty;
- citation-network position;
- source and version status;
- model uncertainty.

Citation counts and venue reputation may describe a record but cannot determine inclusion.

## Screening

Apply explicit eligibility criteria to title/abstract and, where required, full text. Record one primary exclusion reason for systematic runs. Send uncertain or boundary records to the human queue instead of guessing.

## Deduplication

Use exact DOI first, then normalized title, authors, year, identifiers, and version relations. Do not automatically merge preprints, reports, conference papers, and journal versions unless evidence establishes the relation. Preserve provenance from every provider.

## Coverage

Report database, language, date, geography, methods, and publication-type coverage. In focused or systematic mode, assess whether new queries and citation rounds still add eligible concepts, measures, settings, or designs; do not equate zero new famous papers with saturation.
