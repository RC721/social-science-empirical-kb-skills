# Gold Evaluation

Maintain at least 30 human-verified papers spanning common quantitative models and causal designs, qualitative and mixed methods, bibliometrics, database validation, Chinese text, scanned PDFs, abstract-only, and metadata-only records.

For each case store expected identity, evidence level, paper type, key locators, Study/Variable/Model/Finding fields, causal gate, and protected-note checks. Avoid putting copyrighted full papers in the Skill package.

Release gates require zero unsupported identity, key-number, direction, and causal-language errors; no inference beyond metadata-only or abstract-only evidence; and at least 90% agreement for non-critical synthesis fields. Report results by design family and source condition so aggregate performance cannot hide a systematic subgroup failure.

Add fresh forward tasks that were not used to write the patch. Compare against the previous release and reject regressions even if average accuracy rises.
