# Quantitative Design Families

Identify the actual estimator and its purpose, not methods merely mentioned in Related Work.

- Descriptive and associational: cross-tabs, correlations, OLS, GLM, Logit/Probit, Poisson/Negative Binomial, multilevel models, fixed/random effects, SEM, survival and network models.
- Quasi-experimental: DID and event studies, RDD, IV, interrupted time series, synthetic control, matching and weighting. Record assignment or treatment, comparison group, estimand, timing, identifying assumptions, diagnostics, and threats.
- Experimental: randomization unit, allocation, treatment arms, compliance, attrition, analysis population, clustering, and estimand.

Record fixed effects, clustered standard errors, survey or balancing weights, interactions, transformations, sample restrictions, missing-data handling, and robustness only when the paper reports them. PSM or weighting alone does not establish causality; judge identification from design and assumptions.

Distinguish mechanisms tested with measurements or designs from mechanisms proposed only in Discussion. Distinguish mediation from moderation and heterogeneity.
