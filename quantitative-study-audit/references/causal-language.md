# Causal Language Gate

Use three independent fields:

- `author_claim_type`: descriptive, associational, causal
- `design_identification_status`: descriptive, observational, quasi-experimental, experimental, unclear
- `causal_language_gate`: allowed, qualified-only, prohibited

`allowed` requires an experimental or credible quasi-experimental design whose identifying assumptions and implementation support the specific claim. `qualified-only` permits bounded language such as “the design estimates an effect under…” when quasi-experimental identification is plausible but limitations remain. `prohibited` applies to descriptive, observational, or unclear designs and to claims beyond the identified population, treatment, outcome, or period.

Author causal wording is evidence about the author's claim, not proof that the design supports it. Audit reverse causality, confounding, selection, measurement, interference, anticipation, attrition, clustering, functional form, and generalizability as applicable. Keep Codex judgments in the audit artifact and label them explicitly.
