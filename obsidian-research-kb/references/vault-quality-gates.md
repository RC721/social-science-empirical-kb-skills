# Vault Quality Gates

Check:

1. valid YAML and allowed values;
2. unique item keys, DOI normalization, filenames, and active notes;
3. balanced managed blocks and unchanged researcher blocks;
4. valid Evidence IDs for objective details and Findings;
5. evidence-level boundaries for fulltext, abstract-only, and metadata-only;
6. valid Wikilinks, no unintended orphan or duplicate files;
7. manifest counts agree with dashboards, Bases, matrices, maps, and reports;
8. every formal relationship has a permissible type and evidence;
9. every synthesis statement links a claim or paper Finding;
10. a second identical run causes no content changes.

A structural pass does not certify semantic accuracy. Report structural, evidence, semantic-sample, and idempotency results separately.
