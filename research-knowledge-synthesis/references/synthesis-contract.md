# Synthesis Contract

## Inputs

Use only validated evidence, extractions, audits, and verified Literature Notes. Respect exclusion, stale, duplicate, and version states. Do not count two versions of one study as independent evidence.

## Comparison matrix

Each theme includes paper, object, unit, sample, time, place, data, methods, measurements, Findings, evidence boundary, and study limitations. Add domain-specific columns from the active domain config. Preserve “not reported” rather than forcing false comparability.

## Topic review

Write a substantive review organized around questions and claims, not a list of one-sentence paper summaries. Cover scope, conceptualization, measurement, data, designs, robust findings, heterogeneity, conflict, mechanisms, limitations, geography and time, and unresolved gaps. Every synthesis judgment must link a formal claim or a specific paper Finding.

## Gap and question generation

Generate candidate gaps only after checking unresolved boundaries, inconsistent findings, measurement differences, underrepresented populations, weak identification, missing mechanisms, and temporal or geographic omissions. Distinguish absent evidence from evidence of absence. Candidate questions inherit links to the observations that motivated them.
