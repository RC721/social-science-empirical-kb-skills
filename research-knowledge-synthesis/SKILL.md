# Research Knowledge Synthesis

## Required reading

- Read [claim-and-relationship-rules.md](references/claim-and-relationship-rules.md) before creating or changing Claims or paper relations.
- Read [synthesis-contract.md](references/synthesis-contract.md) before creating Concepts, matrices, reviews, or research questions.
- Use [claim-template.md](assets/claim-template.md) and [topic-review-template.md](assets/topic-review-template.md).

## Execute

1. Read Evidence, extraction, method audit, and existing high-quality synthesis.
2. Create one atomic `single-study/preliminary` Claim from one nonduplicate fulltext Finding when useful; state it as that study's result, not consensus.
3. Create or upgrade a multi-study Claim only after checking independent studies, scope, measurement, method, direction, and limiting or conflicting evidence.
4. Create Concepts only for analytically important constructs, recurring operationalizations, or definition disputes.
5. Establish relations only after checking both studies and their Evidence IDs.
6. Build topic-specific comparison matrices before writing reviews.
7. Write reviews around evidence base, theories, designs, data, measurement, findings, heterogeneity, conflicts, boundaries, gaps, and feasible questions.
8. Register dependencies so changed Findings mark affected Claims, Concepts, matrices, and reviews stale.

Do not treat shared keywords, Wikilinks, citation adjacency, or semantic similarity as evidence. Do not collapse incompatible outcomes, populations, estimands, or version-family records into one consensus statement.
