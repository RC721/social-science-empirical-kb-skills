# Paper-type Routing

Assign one primary `paper_type` from:

- quantitative-empirical
- qualitative
- mixed-methods
- bibliometric
- database-validation
- systematic-review
- theoretical-conceptual
- method-framework
- commentary-review

For quantitative studies, produce Study Design, Variable, Model, and Finding Cards and request `$quantitative-study-audit`.

For qualitative studies, extract case selection, participants or documents, fieldwork, coding and interpretive procedures, researcher position where reported, and evidentiary themes. State that an IV–DV frame is inapplicable unless the paper uses one.

For mixed methods, separate components and explain integration. For bibliometric and database validation papers, emphasize corpus construction, coverage, matching, validation, bias, and units. For systematic reviews, preserve protocol, databases, eligibility, screening, synthesis, and risk appraisal. For theoretical or method papers, do not invent samples or variables.
